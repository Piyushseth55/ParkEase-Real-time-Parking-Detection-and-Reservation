About ParkEase

Our Smart Parking System is designed to solve the everyday frustration of finding parking in crowded areas. By integrating advanced technologies like AI-powered cameras and IoT-based sensors, we enable accurate, real-time monitoring of parking spots across both open and covered areas.

The platform offers features such as live availability updates, seamless reservation of parking slots, cost estimation based on duration and type of spot, and interactive spot selection tailored to user preferences. Whether you're looking for a budget-friendly open-roof spot or a secure closed-roof space, we've got it covered.

Our goal is to enhance urban mobility, reduce traffic congestion, and offer a smooth, reliable parking experience. With an intuitive interface and robust backend, our solution empowers users with convenience, control, and efficiency at every step.
